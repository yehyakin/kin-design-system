# Product registers

Select one primary register for the surface being changed. Mixed products may use different registers by route, but one screen should not speak in several visual voices. Every representative workflow also maps its applicable Source, Compare, Decide, Commit, and Recover relationship using `principles/context-thread.md` from the KIN source repository; do not invent phases that the product does not have.

## Information site

Prioritize reading order, provenance, search, navigation, and content density. Use a reading or discovery field with adjacent provenance, without turning the page into a marketing hero or a wall of equal cards.

Read `patterns/information-site.md` from the KIN repository before defining the page model.

## Product workspace

Prioritize entity identity, current state, recent activity, persistent navigation, keyboard efficiency, and Inspector continuity. Use an evidence queue or entity list joined to one dominant investigation field and a decision boundary. Design serves the task and should recede behind the data.

For monitoring, risk, evidence, or investigation products, read `patterns/intelligence-workspace.md`.

## Ecommerce operations

Prioritize product, inventory, campaign, order, channel, and approval state. Join the operating scope and exception queue to selected commercial context and an explicit approval or execution boundary. Make money, quantity, schedule, source, and automation responsibility explicit. Do not confuse a creative preview with an operational record.

Read `patterns/ecommerce-operations.md` before defining commerce states or batch actions.

## Canvas or engineering tool

Prioritize the canvas, selection, mode, coordinates, revision, precision, and reversible editing. Join the dominant canvas to compact tool chrome, selection properties, revision state, and a controlled commit boundary. Chrome remains compact; direct manipulation needs continuous feedback and clear escape paths.

Read `patterns/engineering-canvas.md` before defining tools, selection, units, or generated changes.

## AI-assisted surface

AI does not define a separate visual genre. Apply the relevant product register, then add conclusion, evidence, uncertainty, action, approval, and rollback where AI participates.
